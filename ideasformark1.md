# 💡 Feature Expansion Ideas for CampusConnect

CampusConnect can be evolved into the ultimate daily campus driver. Below is a curated set of features tailored specifically to college student lifestyles, academic workflows, and campus utilities.

---

## 1. 🎓 Academic & Study Features

### 📅 Smart Class Timetable & Sync
*   **Description**: A collaborative schedule manager where class representatives can upload the branch/semester timetable. 
*   **Student Value**: Instantly synced to every student's home page widget with automatic reminders (10 mins before class).
*   **Integrations**: Export to Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar.

### 📊 Attendance Optimizer (75% Threshold Tracker)
*   **Description**: Subject-wise attendance logger that calculates current percentages and indicates how many consecutive classes a student can safely skip or must attend to maintain the VJTI 75% attendance rule.
*   **Student Value**: Prevents year-downs and attendance shortfalls with smart push notifications.

### 📚 PYQ (Previous Year Questions) & Syllabus Hub
*   **Description**: A structured repository of previous years' exam papers, mid-sem solutions, and syllabus manuals indexed by Year -> Branch -> Semester -> Subject.
*   **Student Value**: Saves hours spent hunting for study resources in WhatsApp/Telegram groups during exam weeks.

### ⏱️ Exam Countdown & Study Timer (Pomodoro)
*   **Description**: Dashboard countdown widget for major exams, coupled with a joint virtual study room (Pomodoro session) where friends can join to study together silently.

---

## 2. 🤝 Social & Community Features

### 🤫 Anonymous Confession & Whisper Board
*   **Description**: An anonymous text board where students can share general thoughts, advice, or campus confessions.
*   **Student Value**: High engagement, entertaining, and serves as an outlet for venting, moderated by auto-flagging AI keyword filters.

### 🔔 Smart Match notifications for Lost & Found
*   **Description**: Real-time cross-referencing between reports. If someone reports a "Black wallet" as found and another reported a "Black wallet" as lost, the app triggers an instant notification matching both parties.
*   **Student Value**: Automates the return loop, making the utility significantly more active and helpful.

### 🎭 Club Hub & Event Registrations
*   **Description**: Dedicated sub-pages for official college clubs (e.g., VJTI Racing, Cocoon, DLA) to post recruitments, announcements, and track RSVP registrations.
*   **Student Value**: Centralizes extracurricular updates instead of scattered social media announcements.

---

## 3. 🍔 Campus Life & Utilities

### 🥪 Canteen Pre-Ordering & Queue Skipper
*   **Description**: Digital menu ordering for campus canteens. Students can order, pay via UPI, and receive a push notification when their food is ready for pick-up.
*   **Student Value**: Saves precious minutes during short recess breaks by skipping queue lines.

### 🗺️ Interactive AR Campus Navigator
*   **Description**: A mobile-friendly interactive campus layout or augmented reality (AR) navigation helper to guide freshmen to labs, departments, and hidden campus spots.
*   **Student Value**: Crucial for freshmen navigating complex campus layouts.

### 🚗 Eco-Commute & Carpooling Matcher
*   **Description**: A localized ride-matching board connecting students traveling from similar suburbs or train stations (e.g., Dadar, Matunga, CST).
*   **Student Value**: Cheaper, safer, and eco-friendly travel alternatives.

### 📖 Real-Time Library Occupancy Tracker
*   **Description**: A simple sensor-based or crowd-sourced indicator showing the current busy-ness or empty-seat capacity of silent study rooms and libraries.
